# Waterproofing

Target rain, wet grass, and shallow splash resistance. Do not design V1 for submersion.

## Inside the sealed enclosure

Place:
- Pixhawk
- receiver
- telemetry electronics
- sensitive adapters

Use:
- gasketed polycarbonate box
- correctly sized cable glands
- drip loops
- desiccant
- optional pressure-equalization membrane vent

## Outside

Prefer purpose-built waterproof:
- ESC
- servo
- external connectors

The motor may tolerate incidental water depending on type, but bearings and exposed steel still require drying and maintenance.

## LiPo

Do not permanently seal a LiPo where swelling, heat, or physical damage cannot be inspected. Keep the battery removable and quickly disconnectable.

## Validation

1. Empty enclosure spray test.
2. Put a paper towel or moisture indicator inside.
3. Use only low-pressure spray.
4. Open and inspect.
5. Install electronics only after the enclosure passes.
6. Repeat inspection after the first wet-grass run.
